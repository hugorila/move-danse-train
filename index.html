Meet your instructor

Hi, I’m Manoella! I’ve been teaching dance for over 15 years in Brazil, the UAE and Canada. I’m a CDTA certified ballet teacher, PBT certified teacher, Zumba instructor and certified Ballet Fitness instructor.

My classes are for dancers and non-dancers alike. Whether you want to sweat, stretch, improve your technique or simply enjoy moving to music, you’ll feel welcome. Come as you are!

Good to know

I bought a pass. How do I book?

Tap Book a class, pick your class, then tap Sign in at checkout with the same phone number you used for your pass. No card needed.

Why does the booking page show $25?

That’s the drop-in price. If you sign in and have a pass, it’s applied automatically and you pay $0.

I’ve never danced before. Can I come?

Yes! Every class is open to all levels. Zumba, Ballet Fitness and Mobility/Stretch are great places to start.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and a water bottle. [Footwear for each class]

Can I cancel?

Yes. Please cancel before your class starts, using the link in your confirmation email.

Which studio should I pick?

Griffintown has classes Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday. Saint-Laurent has classes on Friday.
