Find your class

Zumba

Zumba is a fun, energetic dance workout that combines Latin and international music with easy-to-follow choreography. It’s designed for everyone: no dance experience is needed, and you can go at your own pace. Each class is a full-body cardio session that improves endurance, coordination, and mood, while feeling more like a party than a workout.

Ballet Fitness

Ballet Fitness is an intense, full-body circuit that blends aerobic and anaerobic exercise to boost your metabolism, build lean muscle, and sculpt and tone your whole body. Intensity and elegance go hand in hand. Inspired by classical ballet, this method combines resistance, rhythm, and continuous movement to create a high-calorie-burning workout while strengthening your muscles and improving your posture. Every class is designed to shape and refine each detail of your physique. It’s accessible, efficient, and transformative.

Mobility/Stretch

A movement class focused on flexibility, mobility, active range of motion and body awareness. You’ll work through a combination of gentle and active exercises designed to help you move more freely, improve flexibility and build strength and control throughout your range of motion. Suitable for all levels.

Jazz Funk

Jazz Funk is a high-energy dance class that mixes the technique of jazz with the groove and attitude of hip-hop and funk. It’s bold, playful, and powerful, and it’s one of the most fun ways to move your body. Each class starts with a warm-up, followed by jazz and hip hop fundamentals like isolations, grooves, and sharp, strong movement. Then we learn a choreography to current and classic tracks, so you leave with learning a routine and a big smile.

Dance Technique

Have you always wanted to dance, or are you ready to come back to it? This program was created for adults who want to build real technique in a welcoming, supportive environment. Each class blends four styles into one complete experience. Jazz, Contemporary, Lyrical and Classical Ballet. Whether you’re a complete beginner or someone who wants to strengthen and refine your basics, you’ll progress at your own pace with clear guidance and individual corrections. No experience is needed and there is no pressure to be perfect. Just come as you are and enjoy the joy of dancing.

Good to know

I bought a pass. How do I book?

In the schedule, tap the Book button for your day, pick your class, then tap Sign in at checkout with the same phone number you used for your pass. No card needed.

Why does the booking page show $25?

That’s the drop-in price. If you sign in and have a pass, it’s applied automatically and you pay $0.

I’ve never danced before. Can I come?

Yes! Every class is open to all levels. Zumba, Ballet Fitness and Mobility/Stretch are great places to start.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing that lets you move freely. For Zumba and Jazz Funk, we recommend sneakers or other comfortable athletic shoes. For dance technique classes, we recommend dance socks, ballet shoes or jazz shoes. For Ballet Fitness and Mobility/Stretch, we recommend grip socks, ballet shoes or bare feet.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel up to 24 hours before your class starts, using the link in your confirmation email. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before class may not be eligible for a refund or credit.

Which studio should I pick?

Griffintown has classes Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday. Saint-Laurent has classes on Friday. Each day in the schedule has its own booking button, so you always land on the right studio.
